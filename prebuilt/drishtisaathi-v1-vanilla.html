Part 1 · The problem

Every cataract patient has to choose the lens that will stay in their eye for life. Most choose in a few minutes, from an English brochure, in a language they don't speak at home.

No. 1

Cataract is the leading cause of blindness in India.

National Blindness & Visual Impairment Survey, 2015–19
Before surgery

Rushed counselling

Monofocal, toric, EDOF, trifocal. Four very different results, explained in one short consultation.

In the brochure

English and jargon

Dioptre, astigmatism, halos. Words that reach families who think in Kannada, Hindi or Tamil.

After surgery

Calls that never stop

Clinics answer the same questions about drops, pain and precautions, while real warning signs wait in the queue.

So patients pick on price alone, or regret a lens they paid more for.

Build plan

Four phases to a working pilot.

Phase 1

Knowledge & data

  • Clinic protocols in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil
  • Lens fact sheets and safety rules
  • Synthetic IOLMaster-format biometry
Phase 2

Voice + RAG assistant

  • Azure Speech pipeline
  • Answers with source citations
  • Red-flag symptom classifier
Phase 3

Recommend + visualise

  • Lens fit model with SHAP reasons
  • Vision simulator for each lens
  • Shortlist sent to the surgeon
Phase 4

Surgeon loop & testing

  • Approval dashboard
  • Answer-accuracy and safety tests
  • Feedback from doctors and patients
