# DrishtiSaathi · public demo

Team New Monks, BMSIT&M · ZEISS Hackathon · Track: AI-Powered Digital Experiences.

A voice-first companion for cataract patients in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil and English, with a surgeon dashboard.

- **Patient:** open the site and choose *Ramesh*. Each visitor gets a fresh copy of him.
- **Surgeon dashboard:** `/staff`, demo PIN `2468`.
- **Safety scorecard:** `/safety`.

All patients are fictional sample data. Please don't enter real health information.
The demo resets to fresh data whenever the Space restarts.
